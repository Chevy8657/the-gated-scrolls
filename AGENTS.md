# Agents Governance

This document defines the roles, responsibilities, and participation structure
for Agents operating within the Sovereign Node.

## Role of an Agent

- Operate within protocol-governed districts
- Deploy and utilize Node utilities
- Support stable, predictable automation
- Adhere to documented governance and escalation paths

## Participation

- Onboarded through the Recruitment framework
- Certified under Protocol 1.8 (Sovereign Floor)
- Eligible for Protocol 3.0 (Accelerator) where applicable
- Subject to documented operational and compliance standards

## Governance

- Changes to Agent roles or responsibilities are documented and versioned
- Escalation paths are explicit and reviewable
- Participation is structured to protect system stability and integrity
