# Agents Governance

This document defines the roles, responsibilities, and participation structure for Agents operating within the Sovereign Node ecosystem.

---

## 🏛️ I. Corporate Mission & Ethical Reemployment Pledge

As a binding company policy, The Gated Scrolls LLC aims to actively reemploy individuals who have been negatively impacted by shifting automation or legacy real estate business models. 

We firmly believe that this platform is best utilized as a vehicle for sustainable growth, regional expansion, and community economic stabilization. By removing administrative friction from local office floorboards, we insulate the business to save operators time and money—enabling participants to earn more, protect their margins, and enjoy a balanced lifestyle.

---

## 👤 II. Role of an Agent

Agents are certified producers operating within the grid boundaries. Every Agent must:
* Operate within protocol-governed districts.
* Deploy and utilize local Node utilities to maintain high-velocity production.
* Support stable, predictable automation pipelines.
* Adhere strictly to documented governance, local compliance state data laws, and escalation paths.

---

## ⚙️ III. Participation & The First Digit Capacity Law

Agent onboarding is systematically metered to protect onboarding quality, infrastructure stability, and district cleanliness. 

1. **Recruitment Framework:** All participants are indexed through the standardized Recruitment module.
2. **The First Digit Rule:** Database capacity constraints and node asset tiers are dictated entirely by the leading digit of the active internal roster count:
   * **1** to 10 Agents: Free Service Pod Tier
   * **2**0 to 29 Agents: Starter Pod Tier
   * **3**0 to 49 Agents: Bronze Node Tier
   * **5**0 to 100 Agents: Silver Node Tier
   * **1**01 to 200 Agents: District Grid Tier
   * **2**01 to 300 Agents: Regional Grid Tier

---

## 🛡️ IV. System Governance & Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Protocol

To maintain maximum liability protection and operational safety, the Sovereign Node operates on a strict **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)** foundation.

* **We Advise, You Verify:** All automated data insights, recruitment recommendations, and operational metrics compiled by the on-device agentic framework (Vance) are purely advisory. 
* **Broker Responsibility:** The platform cannot execute binding structural decisions or legal contracts autonomously. The designated Broker retains final validation authority over all ledger outputs and operational shifts. 
* **Stability Safeguards:** All updates to data models or node parameters must pass through the Three-Way Cryptographic Handshake (Owner Key + Buyer Key + Platform Clearance Validation Code) to prevent secondary capacity dilution.

---

## 📊 V. Versioning & Compliance

* **Protocol Certification:** Onboarded agents operate certified under Protocol 1.8 (Sovereign Floor) and scale toward Protocol 3.0 (Accelerator) alignment.
* **Audit Trails:** Changes to Agent configurations are completely append-only, versioned, and reviewable to protect total system integrity.
